\vspace{0.8cm}

The Bangladesh Olympiad on Astronomy and
Astrophysics (BDOAA) Problem Booklet is
a compilation of problems from the
Regional and National rounds of the
Olympiad, covering the years 2018--2026.

The booklet aims to preserve problems from
previous editions of the Olympiad and
make them accessible to students,
educators, and astronomy enthusiasts.
